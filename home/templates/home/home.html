The pipeline

One scan starts three things happening at once.

01

Scan to check in

Each student's registered device scans the session QR code. Attendance is logged instantly and tied to that device — no proxy marking.

02

Classify in the background

A lightweight monitor logs foreground app switches during class. The AI model scores each one educational or non-educational, with a confidence value.

03

Surface it live

Scores roll up into a per-student focus number. Teachers see it update on their dashboard; students get a nudge if they drift too long.

About the project

Built for classrooms where the phone is both the tool and the temptation.

FocusFrame doesn't block devices outright — it tells the difference between a student reading lecture notes and one scrolling somewhere else, then hands that distinction to the teacher as a live number instead of a guess.
